# Chapter 1

Mara Venn stepped down from the freight caboose into air that still smelled faintly of rain, though the ground around Red Hollow had already gone back to dust. The train pulled away before she had crossed the cinders, leaving the relay station, a water tank, and a line of poles running west into a pale morning.

A man in a canvas coat waited outside the office. He introduced himself as Eli Rusk, the caretaker, and took one end of her tool case without asking. Mara let him. The case was heavier than it looked, and there was no point proving otherwise before breakfast.

“Storm hit the west span hardest,” Eli said.

Mara looked along the wire. From here the line seemed whole, which meant very little. “Lightning?”

“Close enough to shake the windows.”

Inside, the telegraph key sat quiet beside a cold stove. Mara tried it once and heard nothing from the relay beyond the wall. She checked the office battery indicator, then the local cutout, working through the easy failures before accepting the inconvenient ones.

“Power's here,” she said.

Eli nodded toward the detached signal shed. “Relay's still dead.”

Mara closed her tool case again. If the relay had burned, she could replace contacts. If the line itself had gone to ground somewhere beyond the station, she would spend the morning walking poles.

She glanced west, where the wire disappeared across the wash.

“Show me the relay first,” she said.
